Oral Histories and Indigenous Archival Practices

Written by: Dr. Elena Ramos | March 15, 2024

Indigenous knowledge systems have historically relied on oral tradition as a primary vehicle for transmitting history, governance laws, ecological science, and cultural values across generations.

This paper examines how modern digital archives can respectfully collaborate with community elders to preserve recorded narratives while maintaining Indigenous data sovereignty principles.

We analyze three community-led digital repository projects, highlighting participatory metadata creation and access protocols.
