Water Sovereignty and River Basin Treaties

Dr. Samuel Running Crane • May 05, 2024

Legal personhood granted to rivers represents a paradigm shift in environmental jurisprudence, harmonizing statutory law with ancestral stewardship covenants.

Transboundary watershed agreements are increasingly recognizing tribal governments as primary co-trustees in allocation dialogues.

This article reviews five recent river treaty litigations and the implications for indigenous water rights enforcement.
