Indigenous Cartography: Mapping Ancestral Toponyms

Tariq Whitefeather • June 10, 2024

Counter-mapping initiatives employ GIS technology to reinstate original place names, reflecting deep ecological knowledge embedded in local geography.

By replacing colonial cartographic layers with indigenous toponyms, communities assert territorial memory and historical continuity.

We demonstrate how participatory web maps enable youth to interview elders and geotag sacred landmarks with audio recordings.
